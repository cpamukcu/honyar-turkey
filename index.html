My three answers.

1

“New homes are built after the quake. Is the need already solved?”

I checked quake homes: socket fires in Birecik, 3-day blackouts in Hatay.

Only partly.Istanbul still waits for its quake. About 1.5 million old homes are not renewed.
2

“Do French brands sell earthquake-safe home products?”

I searched Schneider, Legrand, Netatmo and Viko. Only small Turkish firms sell building-level cut-offs, for 6,000 to 10,500 lira.

No.Normal parts, yes. Earthquake-safe for the home, no.
3

“Do the huge new buildings need these products?”

I read the fire rule (BYKHY). Smoke detection inside flats is required only above 51.5 m.

The flats do.Towers are covered. Normal flats are not.

Channels · Honyar's two questions

From factory to home.

Honyar→Distributor→Wholesaler→Shop→Electrician→Family

Pay only after it sells?

2 to 3 months

Not usually. Shops buy on credit. Honyar should give free trial stock to a few dealers.

Makers give 60 to 120 days, secured by cheques, bank guarantees or credit insurance.

How much do they add?

20% and 30%

Wholesalers about 20%. Shops about 30%. One clear price for every dealer.

Market range: wholesaler 15–30%, shop 25–40%. A Trendyol sale costs 15–20% commission.

There are few public numbers. Honyar should check them with dealers in Istanbul.
