Honyar's home strength

The wall switch is a hard market

In China, Honyar is famous for switches and sockets. In Turkey, three big brands already make them in local factories.

Schneider Electric

From France

Makes switches in Turkey (Günsan), €20 million expansion

Legrand

From France

4.68 billion TL of goods made in Turkey in 2024

Viko by Panasonic

Turkish brand, Japanese owner

About 55% of switch sales (2014), 1,200 staff

Made in Turkey or the EU: no extra duty. Chinese breakers and cables: +10–15%.

So Honyar needs a different way in.

Sources: Hürriyet; ISO 500 (2024); Elektrikport; Denetsel (duty rates from 2018, to check)

A · Istanbul renewal

Safe wiring for renewed flats

Owners want safe breakers and cable in their new flat. But the builder usually picks the brands. Lawyers now say: write the brands into the contract.

263,895

homes being renewed in Istanbul right now

Honyar idea

A “Safe wiring” pack: safety breakers, fire-resistant low-smoke cable, water pipes and a smoke sensor.

Source: Ministry of Environment, via TRT Haber

B · New homes in Hatay

Water-safe kitchens and bathrooms

In kitchens and bathrooms, water is close to sockets. A splash can cause an electric shock or a short circuit.

153,755

new homes in Hatay alone

Honyar idea

Splash-proof sockets with a cover, and safety breakers that cut the power in a split second if current leaks. Leak sensors via Mi or Tuya partners.

Source: TOKİ, Dec 2025
