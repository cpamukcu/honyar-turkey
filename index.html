Honyar's home strength

The wall switch is a hard market

In China, Honyar is famous for switches and sockets. In Turkey, three big brands already make them in local factories.

Schneider Electric

From France

Makes switches in Turkey (Günsan), €20 million expansion

Legrand

From France

4.68 billion TL of goods made in Turkey in 2024

Viko by Panasonic

Turkish, Japanese-owned

About 55% of switch sales (2014), 1,200 staff

Made in Turkey or the EU: no extra duty. Chinese breakers and cables: +10–15%.

So Honyar needs a different way in.

Sources: Hürriyet; ISO 500 (2024); Elektrikport; Denetsel (duty rates, 2018)

The open door

Honyar's door: earthquake safety

More than half of fires after big earthquakes are electrical. A home can be ready.

Honyar already makes

Two new ideas for Turkey

Fire-resistant cable

Low smoke, no halogen

Smoke sensor and siren

Loud alarm and a flashing light

Phone alerts

Smart sensors tell the family at once

Seismic breaker

Cuts the power when strong shaking starts

Vibration light

Turns on by itself and lights the way out

Not another switch. An earthquake-safe home.

The two ideas build on Honyar's own breakers and emergency lights. Japan already promotes seismic breakers for this reason.

Sources: Edogawa City (Japan) seismic breaker program; Honyar company profile
